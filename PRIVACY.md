# Privacy Policy — TwoClick for Notion

TwoClick for Notion does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any data.

- The extension runs a content script on Notion pages (`notion.so`,
  `notion.com`, `notion.site`) to intercept link clicks and show a popover.
- It reads a link's URL only at the moment you click it, to display it in the
  popover. When you press **copy link**, that URL is written to your
  clipboard; nothing else is read from or written to the clipboard.
- It makes no network requests, uses no analytics, and stores nothing
  (no cookies, `localStorage`, or `chrome.storage`).
- It requests no extension permissions beyond running on Notion pages.

Source code: https://github.com/ewiner/notion-twoclick

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/ewiner/notion-twoclick/issues
